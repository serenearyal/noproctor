Invisibility, honestly

Hidden where it can be, visible where it can't

We would rather tell you the truth than sell you a surprise. Here is exactly what each platform does.

Windows

Invisible on screen share

The overlay is excluded from screen capture at the OS level, so it does not appear in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, OBS, or a screen recording. Someone watching your shared screen sees your test - not noproctor.

macOS

Same answers, visible overlay

noproctor works the same way and gives the same answers on a Mac, but since macOS 15 Apple no longer lets apps hide from screen capture. On a Mac the overlay is visible in screen shares. If invisibility matters, use Windows.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What exactly is noproctor?

A small desktop app. You press a hotkey, it takes a picture of your screen, sends it to our AI, and shows the answer in a floating window. On Windows that window is hidden from screen sharing and recording.

Is it really invisible?

On Windows, yes - the overlay is excluded from screen capture by the operating system, so it does not show up in Zoom, Teams, Meet, OBS, or a screen recording. On macOS it is not invisible: since macOS 15 Apple no longer lets apps hide from capture, so the overlay is visible in screen shares on a Mac.

Does it work with LockDown Browser or proctoring software?

noproctor is a normal desktop app. It does not disable or get around lockdown browsers or remote proctoring (camera and screen monitoring), and we do not claim it does. The invisibility is specifically about screen sharing and screen recording on Windows.

What subjects and question types work?

Anything that fits on your screen: math, science, reading comprehension, coding, multiple choice, and written responses. If you can see the question, noproctor can read it.

Which platforms are supported?

Windows 10 and 11, and macOS 12 and later. The invisibility feature is Windows-only for the reason above.
