Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

noproctor ("we", "us") makes a desktop application that captures your screen on request and returns an answer. This policy explains what we collect, why, and who it is shared with. We try to keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, and your subscription status.
  • Screen captures: when you press the hotkey, noproctor sends an image of your screen to our servers so our AI model can read the question and produce an answer. The capture is sent for processing; it is not stored on your own computer.
  • Question and answer content: we may retain the text of the questions you submit and the answers returned, to operate, debug, and improve the service.
  • Technical data: app version, basic request logs, an approximate IP address, and a site visit identifier (a cookie) used to understand how people reach us.
  • Payment data: handled by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number.

Why we collect it

  • To return answers to you (the core function).
  • To run your subscription and provide support.
  • To detect abuse, fix bugs, and improve accuracy and speed.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run the service: Amazon Web Services (hosting), Stripe (payments), and an AI model provider that processes your screen captures to generate answers. We do not sell your personal data.

Retention

We keep account data for as long as you have an account. We keep request and content logs for a limited period for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data.

Your choices

You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you can request access to or deletion of your data by emailing us. Deleting your account ends further processing of your captures.

Children

noproctor is intended for users who are old enough to hold their own account and to be responsible for following their institution's rules. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@noproctor.com.
